Rivaled Racing — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Rivaled Racing is a drag-racing game. It is designed to respect your privacy.

Data we collect

None. The app collects no personal information, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no location data. Game progress (garage, cash, records) is stored locally on your device only.

Network access

The app contacts our update service (Capgo) solely to check for and download game updates. Update checks carry a random install ID generated on first launch — it is not linked to you, is not used for tracking, and contains no personal data.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect any.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
